Radios

Any of these runs MeshCore on the US 915 MHz band. Flash it with the MeshCore web flasher; listings may say "Meshtastic", but the hardware is the same. Pick Companion to pair it with your phone, or Repeater for a fixed site, then use the recommended settings.

Reach farther

Antennas and cables

The antenna and its height matter more than anything else. An outdoor antenna on an N connector, fed through a short low-loss cable, makes a fixed node far more useful.

Top pick

A rugged 5 dBi omni antenna for 915 MHz with an N connector and a fiberglass radome. Built for permanent outdoor repeater sites.
